What’s the average auto detailing cost in Oakland in 2026?

Auto detailing prices in Oakland fall into three broad tiers. A basic wash and wax — an exterior hand wash, wheel cleaning, and a quick protective coat — usually runs $120 to $150. These ranges track closely with national and metro-area pricing. Detailing tends to cost more in dense metropolitan markets like the Bay Area than in rural areas, largely because of higher labor and operating costs. How much does interior vs. exterior detailing cost?

Many drivers only need one side of the equation. Interior detailing costs $150 to $350 on average, driven mostly by vehicle size and how much dirt, hair, and staining has built up. A compact sedan in decent shape sits at the low end; a three-row SUV with embedded pet hair and stained upholstery sits at the top.

Exterior detailing costs $120 to $250 for a wash, decontamination, and protective wax or sealant. Add paint correction and the exterior side climbs quickly, because polishing is time-intensive skilled labor. Bundling both into a full detail is almost always cheaper than booking them separately.

💡 Pro Insight

If your car is on a regular maintenance schedule, each detail costs less over time. Dirt, sap, and stains that sit for months take far more labor to remove than the light buildup on a car detailed every few weeks.

What makes auto detailing cost more or less in Oakland?

Two identical-looking cars can get very different quotes. These are the factors that move your price up or down.

💰 Pushes the price down

  • Smaller vehicle (coupe or sedan)
  • Lightly used, regularly maintained
  • Single service instead of a full package
  • No pet hair, stains, or heavy odor
  • Repeat customer on a maintenance schedule

📈 Pushes the price up

  • Large SUV, truck, or RV
  • Heavy dirt, oxidation, or neglected paint
  • Multi-step paint correction plus coating
  • Embedded pet hair, deep stains, smoke odor
  • First-time deep reset after long neglect

Location matters too. Bay Area labor and operating costs are higher than the national average, which is why Oakland detailing typically prices above small-town rates. The upside is convenience — a mobile detailer removes the hidden “cost” of dropping your car at a shop and arranging a ride, since the work happens at your home or workplace.

How much does ceramic coating cost in Oakland?

Ceramic coating is the most premium service most drivers consider, and it’s priced accordingly. In Oakland, expect to invest $500 to $1,800 for a complete professional application. That price includes paint prep, decontamination, paint correction to remove existing swirls, and the multi-year coating itself. Larger vehicles like SUVs and trucks fall toward the higher end because they have more surface area to prep and coat.

Why the jump from a standard detail? A coating is only as good as the prep beneath it, and proper paint correction is skilled, time-consuming work. Professional installation buys durability and a flawless, long-lasting finish — cut-rate coatings tend to fail early. Industry groups like the International Detailing Association emphasize that surface preparation and correct application technique are what separate a coating that lasts years from one that fails in months.
