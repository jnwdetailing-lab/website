Quick Answer

Yes, you can get your car detailed in winter, and in the Bay Area it is a smart time to do it. Interior work doesn't care about the weather, and exterior work just needs a dry window or a covered spot. Winter is when cabins collect wet mud and musty smells, and when paint and glass protection pays off in the rain. If rain lands on your appointment, we reschedule exterior work at no charge.

A lot of people put detailing off until spring, then spend the whole rainy season with muddy mats and a damp-smelling cabin. In the Bay Area the wet months run roughly from November into March, but there are plenty of dry days in between. Here is what is worth doing in winter, how mobile detailing works around the weather, and how to prepare. We do mobile car detailing in Oakland and around the East Bay year-round.

How Mobile Detailing Works Around the Weather

Interior detailing can happen almost any day. Exterior work and coatings need a dry window. Here is how we handle it:

  • We watch the forecast with you. The National Weather Service Bay Area office is a good place to check rain timing a few days out.
  • Exterior services and coatings depend on the weather, so we may reschedule for rain, high wind or extreme heat, at no charge.
  • A garage, carport or covered parking spot makes the forecast matter much less.
  • We bring our own water and power, and you don't need to be home.
  • We finish with a walk-around so you can see the work before we leave.
A Common Winter Mistake

Waiting for spring. By March, months of wet grit have worked into the carpet and a damp smell has settled into the padding, which takes longer to fix than a mid-winter clean. A detail in December or January keeps the cabin dry and fresh through the rest of the season.

How to Prepare for a Winter Appointment

  • Pick the best spot you have. Covered is ideal. If you park on the street, tell us so we can plan around it.
  • Clear out personal items. Gym bags, papers, chargers and loose change. Take car seats out if you can.
  • Tell us what is going on inside. Muddy mats, pet hair, a spilled coffee or a smell you can't place all help us plan the time.
  • Plan for drying time. Shampooed carpet or seats can stay damp for a few hours. Cracking the windows helps.

If you would rather not think about it, a detailing plan brings us back on a monthly, every-two-months or quarterly schedule, rain or shine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you detail a car in the rain?

Interior work, yes, as long as the car is somewhere we can work. Exterior detailing and coatings need dry conditions, so if rain lands on your appointment we reschedule that part at no charge, or work under a garage or carport if you have one.

Is it worth washing my car if it is going to rain again?

Yes. Rain doesn't clean a car; it spreads road film and leaves spots as it dries. Protected paint sheds that much more easily, which is why a sealant or coating is worth having before the wet season.

Why does my car smell musty in winter?

Usually wet carpet padding. Water from shoes and leaks soaks in and doesn't dry. A hot-water extraction pulls it out, and an ozone treatment ({{addon:Ozone odor treatment}}) can help with smells that stay after the cabin is clean.

Do I need to be home for a winter detail?

No. We bring our own water and power. We just need the car somewhere legal with room to open the doors, ideally covered if rain is possible.
