What’s included in a professional ceramic coating in Oakland?

A professional ceramic coating includes four stages: a deep clean and decontamination, paint correction and surface prep, the coating application itself, and curing with a final inspection. Every stage matters — a coating bonds at the microscopic level, so anything left on the paint gets sealed in for years.

Here’s how the process works at J&W Mobile Detailing:

  1. Deep clean & decontamination. Detailed hand wash, clay bar, and iron removal across every exterior surface. Oakland cars collect brake dust from I-880 traffic and industrial fallout near the port — all of it has to come off first.
  2. Paint correction & surface prep. Machine polishing removes swirls and scratches so the coating locks in a flawless finish, not old defects. Our Silver and Gold packages include 1-step and 2-step paint correction for exactly this reason.
  3. Coating application. The ceramic layer is applied panel by panel to every requested surface — paint, trim, rims, or glass.
  4. Curing & final check. The coating bonds and cures, then we inspect everything before handing the vehicle back.

Is ceramic coating worth it compared to waxing?

For most Oakland drivers, yes. A quality wax lasts 6 to 8 weeks before detergents and sun break it down. A professional ceramic coating lasts around 3 years with proper care — one application outlasts roughly 20 wax jobs. It also makes the paint hydrophobic, so water, dirt, and dust slide off and every wash gets easier.

Bay Area conditions make the case stronger. UV exposure fades unprotected paint, salt air works on anything parked near the water, and tree sap in neighborhoods like Rockridge and the Oakland Hills etches clear coat fast. A coating resists all of it. One honest caveat: no coating stops rock chips or deep scratches — that job belongs to paint protection film.

How do you keep a ceramic coating looking new?

Hand wash with a pH-neutral soap and soft microfiber mitts, then dry with clean microfiber towels. That’s it — no more waxing, ever. Stay away from automatic and tunnel washes; their brushes carry grit from other vehicles and are notorious for stripping coatings. Touchless washes are fine.

Pair the coating with an occasional exterior detail and an interior deep clean, and the whole vehicle stays protected inside and out. Don’t just take our word for it — read what Oakland drivers say on Google or browse our reviews page.

How long does ceramic coating last?

A professionally applied ceramic coating lasts around 3 years when properly maintained. Regular hand washing with pH-neutral soap extends its life, while automatic washes, harsh chemicals, and neglected bird droppings shorten it.

No, it isn’t required — but it’s strongly recommended. Correction removes existing swirls and scratches so the coating seals in a flawless finish instead of locking in imperfections. J&W Mobile Detailing’s Silver and Gold packages include 1-step and 2-step correction.
