What Is a Full Detail, Exactly?

A full detail is a complete interior and exterior restoration performed by hand, not a wash with extra steps. It combines every service in an exterior detailing service with every service in an interior detailing package, done in one appointment.

The difference from a car wash comes down to contact and time. A tunnel wash gives your paint about ninety seconds of automated brushes. A full detail gives it hours of hand work, decontamination, protection, and cabin cleaning that reaches under the seats and into the vents. Results last months instead of days.

What’s Included on the Exterior Side?

The exterior half of a full detail includes a two-bucket hand wash, wheel and tire cleaning, clay bar decontamination, iron removal, glass cleaning, and a protective wax or sealant. Each step exists for a reason, and skipping any one of them shows up in the finish.

  • Two-bucket hand wash. One bucket for soapy water, one to rinse the mitt. Grit stays out of the bucket and off your clear coat, this is what prevents the swirl marks tunnel washes leave behind.
  • Decontamination pre-soak. A dwell-time foam that loosens road film, bug residue, and brake dust before anything touches the paint.
  • Wheels, rims, wheel wells, and tires. Baked-on brake dust needs dedicated chemicals and brushes. Fender liners get cleaned too, not just the visible face of the rim.
  • Clay bar treatment. Run your hand over your paint after a wash. If it still feels gritty, that’s bonded contamination. Tar, tree sap, industrial fallout. And only clay pulls it out.
  • Iron contamination removal. Those tiny orange specks on light-colored paint are embedded iron particles. A chemical iron remover dissolves them.
  • Glass and mirrors. Streak-free, including the top inch of the windshield most people miss.
  • Protective wax or paint sealant. The last step, and the one that determines how long the work holds up. Wax gives weeks of gloss; a sealant gives months.
  • Tire dressing. A satin finish, not the greasy shine that slings onto your paint on the first turn.
Oakland-Specific Note

Bay Area cars pick up three things fast: salt-carrying marine air off the estuary, sap and berry drop from Oakland’s street trees, and a fine layer of brake dust from stop-and-go traffic on I-580 and 880. All three etch into unprotected clear coat within weeks, which is why the clay and sealant steps matter more here than in a dry inland climate.

What’s Included on the Interior Side?

The interior half includes a complete vacuum of carpets, seats, and trunk, high-pressure air for crevices, steam cleaning and sanitizing of every surface, glass cleaning, door jambs, console and cup holders, plus leather cleaning and conditioning.

Interior work is where the hours go, and where the tier you pick actually changes the outcome. A baseline interior detail covers the vacuum, surface wipe-down, glass, floor mats, trunk, and door panels. Step up and you add a full shampoo of seats and carpets, that’s the level that pulls coffee spills and years of shoe grime out of the fibers instead of just lifting the loose dirt on top.

The deepest tier adds sanitizing, leather conditioning, and spot stain treatment. High-temperature steam is the tool doing the heavy lifting there: it lifts grime out of textured plastic and sanitizes without soaking your upholstery. If you’ve got pet hair, a car seat that’s seen a toddler, or a lingering smell you can’t place, this is the level worth booking.

What’s Not Included in a Standard Full Detail?

A full detail cleans and protects, it doesn’t repair paint defects. Scratches, swirl marks, oxidation, and long-term coatings are separate services, priced separately, and it’s worth knowing the line before you book.

Is a Full Detail the Right Call for Your Car?

Book a full detail when the car needs both halves, paint that feels rough and an interior that needs more than a vacuum. If only one side is the problem, book that side and save the difference.

Book the Full Detail When

  • Paint feels gritty after a wash, that’s bonded contamination
  • The interior hasn’t been deep-cleaned in six months or more
  • You’re selling, trading in, or ending a lease
  • Pet hair, food spills, or an odor has moved in
  • You’re prepping for ceramic coating (a full detail is required first)

Book a Single Service When

  • The outside looks fine but the cabin is the problem, go interior-only
  • You keep the inside spotless and just want paint protection, go exterior-only
  • The car was fully detailed within the last few months
  • Your real issue is swirl marks, that’s paint correction, not a detail

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between a full detail and a car wash?

A car wash removes loose surface dirt in minutes. A full detail is hours of hand work that decontaminates the paint with a clay bar, protects it with wax or sealant, and deep-cleans the entire interior with steam and shampoo. A wash lasts days; a full detail lasts months.

How often should I get a full detail?

Two to four times a year for most Oakland drivers. Cars parked outside under street trees, daily commuters on 580 or 880, and vehicles carrying kids or pets sit at the higher end. In between, a regular wash keeps sap, dust, and bird droppings off the protected paint.

Does a full detail remove scratches and swirl marks?

No. A full detail cleans, decontaminates, and protects, which makes paint look dramatically better. But swirl marks and scratches sit in the clear coat and only machine polishing removes them. That’s paint correction, offered as an add-on to the detail.

Can you do a full detail if I park on the street?

Usually yes. We need a legal, reasonably flat spot with room to open the doors and move around the vehicle. Driveways are easiest, but plenty of our Oakland appointments happen at the curb. Send a photo of your spot when you book if you’re unsure.

Should I get a full detail before ceramic coating?

Yes, it’s required. A coating bonds to whatever is on the paint, so the surface has to be washed, decontaminated, and clay-barred first, and usually polished too. Any legitimate Oakland ceramic coating quote includes that prep work.
