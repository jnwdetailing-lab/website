What Is a Full Detail, Exactly?

A full detail is a complete interior and exterior clean done by hand, not a wash with extra steps. It combines an exterior detail with a full interior detail, done in one appointment.

The difference from a car wash comes down to contact and time. A tunnel wash gives your paint about ninety seconds of automated brushes. A full detail gives it hours of hand work, protection, and cabin cleaning that reaches under the seats and into the vents. Results last weeks instead of days.

What’s Included on the Exterior Side?

The exterior half of our Full Detail is a pre-soak, a professional hand wash, a high-gloss spray wax, wheels and tires, trim, door jambs and exterior windows. Clay bar decontamination and a longer-lasting ceramic sealant are add-ons, because not every car needs them.

  • Pre-soak. A dwell-time foam that loosens bugs, bird droppings, road film, and brake dust before anything touches the paint.
  • Professional hand wash. Clean mitts and a two-bucket method keep grit off your clear coat. This is what prevents the swirl marks tunnel washes leave behind.
  • Wheel wells, rims, and tires. Wheel wells are pressure-washed, rim faces and tires are cleaned with dedicated brushes and chemicals, and tires get a satin dressing, not the greasy shine that slings onto your paint on the first turn.
  • Trim, door jambs, and windows. Exterior trim dressed, door jambs wiped out, and every window streak-free, including the top inch of the windshield most people miss.
  • High-gloss spray wax. The last step. It adds gloss and a few weeks of protection, so water beads and dirt rinses off.
  • Clay bar treatment (add-on, {{addon:Clay bar treatment}}). Run your hand over your paint after a wash. If it still feels gritty, that’s bonded contamination: tar, tree sap, industrial fallout. Only clay pulls it out.
  • Ceramic sealant upgrade (add-on, {{addon:Ceramic sealant upgrade (6 months)}}). Swaps the spray wax for a ceramic sealant that protects for about six months.
Oakland-Specific Note

Bay Area cars pick up three things fast: salt-carrying marine air off the estuary, sap and berry drop from Oakland’s street trees, and a fine layer of brake dust from stop-and-go traffic on I-580 and 880. All three etch into unprotected clear coat within weeks, which is why the clay bar and ceramic sealant add-ons earn their keep here more than in a dry inland climate.

Is a Full Detail the Right Call for Your Car?

Book a full detail when the car needs both halves: paint that needs a proper wash and protection, and an interior that needs more than a vacuum. If only one side is the problem, book that side and save the difference.

Book the Full Detail When

  • The interior hasn’t been deep-cleaned in six months or more
  • You’re selling, trading in, or ending a lease
  • Pet hair, food spills, or an odor has moved in (add the matching add-on)
  • Paint feels gritty after a wash (add the clay bar)
  • You want the car clean all year (put it on a plan)

Book a Single Service When

  • The outside looks fine but the cabin is the problem: go interior only
  • You keep the inside spotless and just want the outside done: go exterior only
  • The car was fully detailed within the last few months
  • Your real issue is swirl marks: that’s paint correction, not a detail

Frequently Asked Questions

What’s the difference between a full detail and a car wash?

A car wash removes loose surface dirt in minutes. A full detail is hours of hand work: the whole interior vacuumed, steam-cleaned and stain-treated, leather conditioned, and the outside hand washed, waxed and finished down to the door jambs. A wash lasts days; a full detail lasts weeks.

How often should I get a full detail?

Two to four times a year for most Oakland drivers. Cars parked outside under street trees, daily commuters on 580 or 880, and vehicles carrying kids or pets sit at the higher end. If you want it clean all year, a plan brings us back monthly, every two months or every three months at a lower price per visit.

Does a full detail remove scratches and swirl marks?

No. A full detail cleans and protects, which makes paint look dramatically better. But swirl marks and scratches sit in the clear coat and only machine polishing removes them. That’s paint correction: a one-step polish is a {{addon:Polish}} add-on to the detail.

Can you do a full detail if I park on the street?

Usually yes. We need a legal, reasonably flat spot with room to open the doors and move around the vehicle. Driveways are easiest, but plenty of our Oakland appointments happen at the curb. Send a photo of your spot when you book if you’re unsure.

Do I need a full detail before a ceramic coating?

The paint has to be washed, decontaminated, and clay-barred first, and usually polished too. Our ceramic coating packages include all of that prep, so you don’t need to book a separate detail. Any legitimate coating quote should include it.
